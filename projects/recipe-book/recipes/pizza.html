    • Cheese Pizza Reciepe

    Description:

    Cheese pizza is a classic comfort food loved for its simple yet delicious combination of flavors. It features a warm, crispy crust topped with rich tomato sauce

    and a generous layer of melted cheese. The golden, bubbly cheese adds a savory, creamy taste that pairs perfectly with the slightly tangy sauce and crunchy crust,

    making cheese pizza a satisfying meal for people of all ages.

    To make a homemade cheese pizza, start by preparing or using ready-made pizza dough and spreading it evenly on a baking sheet or pizza pan. Add a layer of tomato pizza sauce,

    then generously sprinkle shredded mozzarella cheese over the top. For extra flavor, add a small amount of Italian seasoning or dried oregano. Bake the pizza in a preheated

    oven at about 450°F (230°C) for 10 to 15 minutes, or until the crust is golden brown and the cheese is melted and bubbly. Let it cool for a few minutes before slicing and serving warm.

    Ingredients:

    • 1 pizza dough
    • 1 cup tomato pizza sauce
    • 2 cups shredded mozzarella cheese
    • 1 tablespoon olive oil
    • 1 teaspoon Italian seasoning
    • ½ teaspoon garlic powder
    • A small pinch of salt
    • Flour for dusting

    Steps:

    1. Preheat the oven to 450°F (230°C).
    2. Lightly flour a clean surface and roll out the pizza dough.
    3. Place the dough on a lightly greased pizza pan or baking sheet.
    4. Spread the tomato sauce evenly over the dough.
    5. Sprinkle the shredded mozzarella cheese generously over the sauce.
    6. Add Italian seasoning and garlic powder for extra flavor.
    7. Lightly brush the crust with olive oil.
    8. Bake for 10 to 15 minutes, or until the crust is golden brown and the cheese is melted and bubbly.
    9. Remove the pizza from the oven and let it cool for a few minutes.
    10. Slice and serve warm.
