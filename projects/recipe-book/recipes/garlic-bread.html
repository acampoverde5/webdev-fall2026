Garlic Bread Reciepe

Description:

Garlic bread is a delicious and comforting side dish made with crispy bread, creamy butter, and flavorful garlic. When baked, the bread becomes golden and

slightly crunchy on the outside while remaining soft and buttery on the inside. It is a simple and flavorful addition to pasta, pizza, soups, and many other meals.

To make garlic bread, start by slicing a loaf of French or Italian bread in half lengthwise. Mix softened butter with minced garlic, parsley, and a little salt,

then spread the mixture evenly over the cut sides of the bread. Place the bread on a baking sheet and bake it in a preheated oven at 400°F (200°C) for about 10 to 12 minutes,

or until the edges are golden and crispy. For cheesy garlic bread, sprinkle mozzarella or Parmesan cheese on top before baking. Once finished, slice the bread and serve it warm.

Ingredients:

  • 1 loaf of French or Italian bread
  • ½ cup softened butter
  • 3 to 4 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 tablespoon fresh parsley, chopped
  • ¼ teaspoon salt
  • ¼ teaspoon black pepper
  • ½ cup shredded mozzarella or Parmesan cheese

Steps:

  1. Preheat the oven to 400°F (200°C).
  2. Slice the loaf of bread in half lengthwise.
  3. Mix the softened butter, minced garlic, parsley, salt, and black pepper in a bowl.
  4. Spread the garlic butter evenly over the cut sides of the bread.
  5. Sprinkle mozzarella or Parmesan cheese on top if desired.
  6. Place the bread on a baking sheet.
  7. Bake for 10 to 12 minutes, or until the bread is golden and crispy around the edges.
  8. Remove the bread from the oven and let it cool for a minute or two.
  9. Slice into pieces and serve warm.
