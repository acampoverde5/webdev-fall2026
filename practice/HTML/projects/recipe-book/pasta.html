Alfredo Pasta Reciepe

Description:

Alfredo pasta is a creamy and comforting Italian-inspired dish made with tender pasta coated in a rich, smooth Alfredo sauce.

The sauce combines butter, cream, and Parmesan cheese to create a deliciously creamy texture and savory flavor.

It is an easy and satisfying meal that can be enjoyed on its own or served with chicken, shrimp, or vegetables.

To make Alfredo pasta, begin by cooking the pasta in a pot of salted boiling water until it is tender. While the pasta cooks, melt butter in a

large pan and add heavy cream, stirring until the mixture is warm and smooth. Gradually add grated Parmesan cheese and continue stirring until

the cheese melts and the sauce becomes creamy. Season with garlic, salt, and black pepper, then add the cooked pasta and toss until it is evenly coated.

Serve the pasta warm with extra Parmesan cheese sprinkled on top.

Ingredients:

  • 12 oz fettuccine or other pasta
  • 2 tablespoons butter
  • 1 cup heavy cream
  • 1 cup grated Parmesan cheese
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • ½ teaspoon salt
  • ½ teaspoon black pepper
  • Fresh parsley for garnish

Steps:

  1. Bring a large pot of salted water to a boil.
  2. Add the pasta and cook according to the package instructions until tender.
  3. Drain the pasta and set it aside.
  4. Melt the butter in a large pan over medium heat.
  5. Add the minced garlic and cook for about 30 seconds.
  6. Pour in the heavy cream and stir until heated through.
  7. Gradually add the Parmesan cheese, stirring until it melts and the sauce becomes creamy.
  8. Season with salt, black pepper, and Italian seasoning if desired.
  9. Add the cooked pasta to the sauce and toss until it is completely coated.
  10. Serve warm and garnish with extra Parmesan cheese and fresh parsley.
